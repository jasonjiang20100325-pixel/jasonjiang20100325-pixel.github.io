03 / THE REFORM

Let results matter.

A more open basis for tournament entry.

2023 announcement, 2025 implementation

Valve announced changes to reduce business relationships deciding access to top events. The new requirements began in 2025.

Valve Regional Standings (VRS) ranks teams using competition results and helps determine tournament invitations.
