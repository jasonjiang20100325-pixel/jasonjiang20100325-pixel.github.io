Grid Games

We made two games for our CS project.

Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time. If they match, they stay face up. Find all 8 pairs and see how many flips you used.

Play Memory Match

Robot Maze

Get the robot to the flag. Press m to open the editor, pick an example or write your own code, then click Run.

You get 2 points for finishing in 40 steps or less, or 1 point for finishing in 41 to 200 steps. Turning counts as a step.
